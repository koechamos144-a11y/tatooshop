Good to know

Common questions

Do I need to pay a deposit?

Yes — a $50 deposit books your slot and comes straight off your final price. Reschedule at least 48 hours ahead and it moves with you.

How should I prepare for my appointment?

Eat a proper meal an hour before, sleep well, skip alcohol for 24 hours, and wear clothing that gives easy access to the area. Bring ID — we can't tattoo or pierce anyone under 18.

Is the price negotiable?

Our rates are fixed and reflect the artist's time and materials. For larger projects we can split the work across sessions to spread the cost.

What if I don't like the design?

You'll see the design before we start. We'll adjust it as many times as needed in that session — you never get tattooed without approving the artwork first.

Do you do walk-ins?

Yes, subject to availability. Small tattoos and piercings are usually fine same-day. Call ahead to check the artist's schedule.
